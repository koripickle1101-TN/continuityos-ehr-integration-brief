# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 1: Workflow Discovery

Map the full workflow from patient check-in through billing. Interview front-desk staff, nurses, providers, coders, billing specialists, and patient-facing teams to identify delays, duplicate work, and documentation gaps.

## Phase 2: Requirements Definition

Define clinical, operational, patient experience, and revenue cycle requirements. Requirements should include usability, interoperability, reporting, documentation support, charge capture, and implementation support.

## Phase 3: System Evaluation

Use a structured scorecard to compare EHR modules, communication tools, patient flow tools, and revenue cycle support features.

## Phase 4: Configuration and Testing

Configure templates, interfaces, workflows, alerts, and reporting tools. Test using realistic patient scenarios before go-live.

## Phase 5: Training and Go-Live Support

Provide role-specific training for front-desk staff, clinical staff, coding teams, billing staff, and managers. Use at-the-elbow support during go-live.

## Phase 6: Optimization

Collect feedback after launch and refine templates, alerts, reports, order sets, and communication workflows.

## Phase 7: Outcome Review

Review performance at 30, 90, and 180 days after implementation.

## Implementation Principle

Technology should support the way care is actually delivered. The roadmap should reduce friction for patients and staff instead of creating another layer of disconnected work.
